Risk tracker

How risk is calculated

Contract lock rules

  • Cash — locks futures and basis
  • Basis — locks basis only (futures still open)
  • DP — locks neither
  • Everything else (forward, HTA, min price, …) — locks futures only

Per crop:

  • Futures open = expected bu − futures-locked bu
  • Basis open = expected bu − basis-locked bu
  • Futures risk $ = futures open × futures risk $/bu
  • Basis risk $ = basis open × basis risk $/bu
  • Money at risk = (bin bu + unsold) × local cash mark
