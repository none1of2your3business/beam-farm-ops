Cost of carry

Every figure is editable · shrink is built from ISU AgDM A2-33

Interest is the money tied up in the grain. Storage is the bin or elevator fee. Shrink is bushels you lose: handling/quality over time, plus extra moisture if you dry below the sale standard to keep it. Formula: interest + storage + handling shrink over the hold, plus one-time extra-moisture shrink and in-and-out handling.
